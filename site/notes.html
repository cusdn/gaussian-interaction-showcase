Evidence, scope and sources.

Separate assets and measurements

  • The robot / part contact scene uses 239,941 supplied public Bonsai Gaussians and SH0 appearance.
  • A separately trained Bonsai baseline has 2,453,898 Gaussians, 255 training / 37 same-capture image-loss holdout views, 31.437 dB PSNR / 0.9364 SSIM. Supplied COLMAP uses the capture. Later development reused these views.
  • Complete third-order SH / HDR, numerical checks and later actual DX11 / UDP validation belong to a separate trained-model UE candidate. The included older UE supplied-scene recording is labeled separately.
  • The 99.4% figure measures executed post-grasp drawer goal reaching within 20 mm, not Bonsai operation, complete autonomous RLBench task success, image reconstruction or real-robot transfer. Thirty configurations, 60 goals and repeated method / seed labels produce 960 replays.
  • The 35.68 → 7.71 nominal mm figure is marker prediction for an unseen oblique push in a 64-case simulated material benchmark, not a real-plant calibrated error.

Controls and retained failures

Robot contact has paired same-command / disabled-contact controls. Material probing includes single-probe and fixed-pair controls; a frozen changed-contact follow-up fails the overall cross-condition advantage test. The offline Gaussian study retains weak reconstruction and action-effect ablations. Real pushing improves interpolation but loses to physics under angle and contact-side shifts.

Native UE controls replay recorded MuJoCo state. Playback speeds do not alter physical parameters or re-solve collision. Known-state block stacking / domino demonstrations do not establish a visual policy or learned predictor. Gaussian appearance retains baked illumination; dynamic Gaussian self-shadowing and general relighting remain open.

Project contribution

Project work spans representation and coordinate contracts, part association, geometry / collision engineering, Panda contact, native rendering / UDP interfaces, camera / depth repairs, controlled experiment variants, ablations and independent metrics. Established Gaussian representations, engines and upstream research are credited below. Critical new method novelty, full original policy training reproduction and universal physical correctness are not claimed.
